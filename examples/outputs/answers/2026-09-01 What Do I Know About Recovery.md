# What Do I Know About Recovery

## Question

What does the vault already say about recovery and injury prevention, before I go
looking for anything new?

## Answer

Three claims, all consistent:

1. **Adaptation happens during recovery, not during effort.** A skipped rest day
   costs more than a skipped session ([[Progressive Overload]]).
2. **Back-to-back hard days are the specific mechanism of failure** in this kind of
   plan, and match my own week 7 experience
   ([[2026-08-15 Base Training for Distance Runners]]).
3. **The debt is invisible until it is due**, and lands somewhere other than where
   it was taken on ([[Training Load Mirrors Technical Debt]]).

Together these argue for structure over discipline: place the easy days so that
back-to-back hard days are impossible, rather than relying on judgment when tired.

## What I could not find

Nothing on sleep, and nothing on nutrition. Both are almost certainly load-bearing
for recovery and the vault is silent on them — that is a gap, not a conclusion.
Also unresolved: whether the 10% weekly ceiling is a constant or a function of prior
training (see the `## Disagreements` section on [[Progressive Overload]]).
