# Obsidian

## What it is

A local-first Markdown editor that treats a folder of plain files as a linked
knowledge base. Notes are files; links are `[[wikilinks]]` resolved by name.

## Why it keeps coming up

It is the reason this vault is portable. Nothing here depends on the editor: the
files are Markdown, the metadata is YAML, and the scripts are plain Python. If
Obsidian disappeared, the vault would still be a vault.

## Timeline

- 2026-08-19 — Chose it over alternatives specifically because the storage format
  is not owned by the tool.
