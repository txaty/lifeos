# Training Load Mirrors Technical Debt

## The connection

Accumulated training load and accumulated technical debt behave the same way: both
are borrowed capacity that feels free while it is being taken on, is repaid on a
schedule you do not choose, and produces failures that look sudden but were not.

## Why it holds

- Both compound quietly and are invisible in any single day's measurements.
- Both are repaid during periods that produce nothing visible — rest weeks,
  refactoring — which is exactly why both get skipped.
- In both, the failure lands somewhere other than where the debt was taken on: the
  calf gives out, not the quad; the deploy breaks, not the module you rushed.

## Where it breaks

The analogy fails on reversibility. Tissue heals on its own if you stop; code does
not improve while you ignore it. Rest is a real repayment mechanism for one and a
no-op for the other.
