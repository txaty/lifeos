# Weekly Review Practice

## What it is for

Keeping a system honest without adding a second system to maintain it. One recurring
appointment where the facts get looked at and the lies get corrected.

## Steps

1. Empty the inbox. Every item goes somewhere or is deleted.
2. Read the facts before forming an opinion: `python3 scripts/vault.py status`.
3. For each stale project, decide: activate, pause with a stated unblock condition,
   or delete. "Leave it" is not on the list.
4. Record decisions that have consequences as their own note.
5. Write three lines: what went well, what got in the way, next week's one priority.

## When it works

- When it happens at a fixed time attached to something already in the week.
- When the facts are gathered by a script, so the review argues with data instead
  of with mood.

## When it does not

- When it becomes a planning session. Planning expands; review should contract.
- When more cadences get added. Monthly and quarterly reviews are usually a sign
  the weekly one is not being trusted.
