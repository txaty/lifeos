# Spaced Repetition

## What it is

Recall is strengthened most when it is attempted just as it starts to fail. Reviewing
at widening intervals therefore beats reviewing often, for the same total effort.

## Why it matters

It is the reason a second brain has to be re-read rather than only written. Notes
you never revisit decay the same way memory does, just more slowly.

## Key points

- Difficulty of recall at review time is the signal, not time elapsed.
- Re-reading is much weaker than attempting recall first.
- The effect is about retrieval, not about storage.

## In practice

- Let the weekly review surface old pages rather than scheduling them by hand.
- When a page is easy to recall, widen the interval instead of deleting it.
